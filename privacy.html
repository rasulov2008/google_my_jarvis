Privacy Policy for MyBotJarvis

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information Collection

MyBotJarvis is a personal Telegram bot. It uses Google OAuth to access Google Services on behalf of the user. We only access the specific data scopes (e.g., Google Calendar, Gmail, etc.) that the user explicitly grants permission for during the login process.

2. Use of Data

The data accessed is used exclusively to provide the bot's functionality to the user (e.g., reading schedules or sending notifications). The data is processed in real-time and is not stored on external servers, except for the necessary authentication tokens stored securely by the user.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data or Google user data with any third parties. The data is strictly used for the personal integration between the Telegram bot and your Google account.

4. Revoking Access

You can revoke the bot's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact the developer at: your-email@gmail.com
